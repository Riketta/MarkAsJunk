# Mark as Junk

Mark any item as junk and let your colonists do the sorting: while a junk dump exists on a map, haulers keep junk out of your stockpiles and carry it to dumps instead. Nothing is marked by default - you decide what counts as junk.
Precise per-item control.

## What it does

- Every haulable thing gains a toggle next to the vanilla allow/forbidden button - loot, corpses, minified furniture, anything from any mod or DLC. It behaves exactly like forbidding: stack splits keep the mark, junk never merges into non-junk stacks, an optional hotkey is available.
- Select a stockpile zone or storage building and toggle "Junk dump": it keeps working as normal storage and additionally accepts junk, while all other storage on the map rejects junk - so pawns actively sort it out. Remove the last dump and junk simply flows back.
- Optional auto-marking: a stockpile can mark its contents on arrival like a conveyor, and apparel a colonist takes off because it no longer passes their outfit policy is marked automatically.
- Electric smelters gain "smelt/destroy junk" bills that consume whole stacks and feed exclusively on marked items.
- The junk marker is attached to every haulable thing at runtime - works with all mods by design, safe to add or remove at any time.
